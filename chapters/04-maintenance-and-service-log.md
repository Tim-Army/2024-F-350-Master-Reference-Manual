# 4. Maintenance & Service Log

## Maintenance framework

- Use the official Owner's Manual schedule as the primary source for normal and severe-duty intervals.
- Record date, mileage, parts used, fluid quantity, receipts, and any diagnostic results.
- Future additions: oil analysis, tire wear, alignment reports, battery testing, recalls, warranty work, and scale tickets.

## Scheduled maintenance - 7.3L gasoline

Added 9 October 2026. The printed owner's manual (REF-001 p. 590) and the workshop manual both point to Ford's online schedule; this is transcribed from Ford's current Super Duty owner's manual: [Normal Scheduled Maintenance](https://www.fordservicecontent.com/Ford_Content/vdirsnet/OwnerManual/Home/Content?variantid=7647&languageCode=en&countryCode=USA&Uid=G2069997&ProcUid=G2070001&userMarket=USA&div=f&vFilteringEnabled=False&buildtype=web) and [Special Operating Conditions Scheduled Maintenance](https://www.fordservicecontent.com/Ford_Content/vdirsnet/OwnerManual/Home/Content?variantid=7647&languageCode=en&countryCode=USA&moidRef=G1612531&Uid=G2117148&ProcUid=G2117149&userMarket=usa&div=f&vFilteringEnabled=False&buildtype=web). **Gasoline engine items only** - the diesel rows (fuel filters, DPF, foam filter) do not apply.

### Oil changes - the Intelligent Oil-Life Monitor

The truck tells you when to change the oil (information display / FordPass oil-life %). **Never exceed 10,000 mi, 450 engine hours or 12 months.** Ford's guide to what to expect:

| Use | Message typically at |
| --- | --- |
| Normal - highway commuting, no or moderate load or towing | 7,500-10,000 mi |
| Severe - moderate to heavy load or towing, mountains, extended idling or heat/cold | 5,000-7,500 mi |
| Extreme - maximum load or towing, extreme heat or cold | 2,500-5,000 mi |

Each hour of idling counts as about 25 mi. **Reset the monitor after every oil change.**

### Normal schedule

**Every 10,000 mi / 450 engine hours / 12 months, whichever comes first:**

- Change the engine oil and filter (or sooner, at the oil-life message).
- Rotate the tires, inspect wear and measure tread depth.
- Inspect the wheels and related components for noise, wear, looseness or drag.
- Inspect the brake pads, rotors, linings, hoses and parking brake.
- Inspect the coolant strength, freeze protection, level and hoses.
- Inspect the exhaust system and heat shields.
- Inspect the front axle and U-joints (4x4); grease fittings if fitted.
- Inspect the steering linkage, ball joints, suspension, tie-rod ends, driveshaft and U-joints; grease fittings if fitted.
- Multi-point inspection (recommended).

**Other items:**

| Interval | Service |
| --- | --- |
| Every 3 years | Change the brake fluid (dealer equipment) |
| Every 20,000 mi | Replace the cabin air filter (Motorcraft FP-92) |
| Every 30,000 mi | Replace the engine air filter (Motorcraft FA-1950) |
| Every 60,000 mi | Repack front wheel bearings - non-sealed bearings only; **not this truck** (sealed 4x4 hubs) |
| Every 100,000 mi | Replace the spark plugs |
| Every 100,000 mi | Inspect the accessory drive belt(s); if not replaced, inspect every 15,000 mi after |
| Every 150,000 mi | Change the automatic transmission fluid and filter; replace the drive belt(s) if not replaced in the last 100,000 mi; change the axle fluid (see the exception below); change the transfer case fluid |
| 200,000 mi or 10 years | First engine coolant change, then every 100,000 mi or 5 years |

### Towing schedule - once the fifth wheel is in regular use

Ford: follow these when you **primarily** tow; occasional towing needs no extra maintenance. Full-time travel with the fifth wheel counts as primarily towing.

| Interval | Service |
| --- | --- |
| Inspect frequently | U-joint grease fittings, if fitted |
| Every 5,000 mi | Inspect the wheels and related components; **rotate the tires** and measure tread depth |
| Every 5,000 mi or 6 months | **Change the engine oil and filter**; lubricate U-joint fittings, if fitted |
| Every 30,000 mi | Repack front wheel bearings - non-sealed bearings only; **not this truck** (sealed 4x4 hubs) |
| Every 60,000 mi | **Change the transfer case fluid** (4x4) and **replace the spark plugs** |

Ford has similar extra-maintenance lists for dusty or unpaved roads and off-road use (5,000 mi / 6-month oil changes, and engine and cabin air filters replaced as needed).

**Axle exception** (REF-001 p. 592): axle fluid needs no change unless leaking or submerged. But for **long periods of towing above 70°F**, or long wide-open-throttle runs above 45 mph, **change the rear axle fluid every 30,000 mi if it is non-synthetic.** That is waived - back to 150,000 mi - if the axle holds **75W-140 synthetic** (WSL-M2C192-A, Motorcraft XY-75W140-QL). **This truck's 11.6 in axle is factory-filled with 75W-140 synthetic** to WSL-M2C192-A ([chapter 6](06-fluids-and-capacities.md)), and no axle service is recorded, so **the 30,000 mi interval is waived and 150,000 mi applies**. Keep it that way: any axle service must refill with the same synthetic (plus friction modifier only if the axle is a Traction-Lok - this one is the electronic locker).
