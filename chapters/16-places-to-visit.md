# 16. Places to Visit

A running list of destinations for the truck, and later the truck with a trailer. Add a place to the **wish list** when it comes up. Move it to **visited** after the trip, with anything worth knowing for next time.

## Rig limits for route and site planning

Check a destination and its approach roads against these figures before committing to it.

| Limit | Truck alone | With a fifth wheel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Height | **82.5 in (6 ft 10.5 in)**, measured ([chapter 1](01-vehicle-information.md)) | Set by the trailer. The toy haulers screened so far run **12 ft 0 in to 13 ft 5 in** tall ([chapter 15](15-fifth-wheel-candidates.md)); measure the chosen trailer with its A/C |
| Length | **22 ft 2 in** bumper to bumper ([chapter 1](01-vehicle-information.md)) | Trailer capped at **35 ft** ([chapter 15](15-fifth-wheel-candidates.md)); measure the combined length once hitched |
| Weight | 11,900 lb GVWR | See [chapter 10](10-fifth-wheel-and-towing.md) for combined ratings and scale weights |

What to check for each place:

- **Campground site length** against the combined length, plus room to swing in. Many state and national park sites were built for shorter rigs.
- **Low clearances** on the route: bridges, tunnels, fuel station canopies and drive-throughs.
- **Road restrictions**: grade, switchbacks, and vehicle-length limits on scenic roads. Some park roads ban vehicles over a set length.
- **Fuel stops** on remote stretches, especially when towing, when range drops.

## Trailer length and state parks - 35 ft against ~40 ft

Added 26 September 2026. The extra 5 ft is a large step down in what can be booked: 35 ft is about where most state park sites top out, and above that the choice narrows sharply.

**How much each fits.** No national dataset exists for state parks. The best published figure is for national park campgrounds ([Camper Report](https://camperreport.com/best-rv-length-for-national-parks/)):

| Trailer length | National park campgrounds that fit it |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 35 ft | **73%** |
| Up to 40 ft | **53%** |

State parks follow the same pattern and are often stricter. Many older loops cap sites at **30-35 ft**; newer or renovated loops and full-hookup pull-throughs are where the long sites are ([America's State Parks](https://www.americasstateparks.org/rv-sizes-at-state-parks/)).

**Check what a published length measures.** Reservation systems ask for the trailer's **overall** length, front cap to rear bumper. Fifth-wheel specs are often **hitch to rear** - Grand Design's are - so the front cap over the truck bed adds roughly **2-3 ft**. A Momentum 350G listed at 39 ft 9 in is about **42 ft overall** on a reservation form, and a trailer listed at 35 ft pin-to-rear is about 37-38 ft. Confirm or measure before booking.

**Combined length with this truck.** Front bumper to kingpin is about **17 ft 8 in** (176 in wheelbase plus ~40 in front overhang, less a few inches for the kingpin ahead of the rear axle):

| Trailer, pin to rear | Truck front to trailer rear |
| --- | --- |
| 35 ft | **~53 ft** |
| 39 ft 11 in | **~58 ft** |

This matters on back-in sites where the truck must fit on the pad, and on tight loop roads. Many sites expect the truck parked beside the trailer or in a separate spot, so the pad usually needs to cover only the trailer.

**In practice:**

- **35 ft** books most modern state park sites and many older ones, with more choice on holiday weekends.
- **~40 ft (about 42 ft overall)** means fewer parks and sites, booked earlier. Read each campground's site list for its maximum equipment length rather than the park-wide limit, and watch for tight loops, trees and low limbs - several candidates in [chapter 15](15-fifth-wheel-candidates.md) are 13 ft 2 in to 13 ft 6 in tall.
- **Filtering:** ReserveAmerica, the state reservation portals and recreation.gov filter by equipment length. Reviews on The Dyrt and Campendium often flag sites that are longer or shorter than listed.

This is the trade-off behind the **35 ft hard limit** and the separate **40 ft list** in chapter 15.
