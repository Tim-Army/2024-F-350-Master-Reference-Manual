# 12. 3D-Printed Accessories

## Design library

- Preferred materials for interior vehicle parts: PETG, ASA, or ABS rather than PLA because of heat exposure.
- Candidate projects: center-console organizer/divider, under-seat modular bins, fuse kit, trailer-adapter holder, recovery-gear organizer, compressor organizer, hitch-pin rack, and bed tool clips.
- Document each design with project ID, material, printer settings, hardware, STL/CAD filename, revision notes, fitment, and installation photos.
