# 7. Electrical Atlas

## APIM / BCM rear-camera configuration

- Reference REF-008 covers enabling or disabling an OEM rear camera kit through APIM and BCM configuration.
- Recorded post-programming verification: complete APIM programming; switch ignition off; open a front door and allow cluster/display power-down; switch ignition on; select Reverse with brake applied and parking brake engaged to verify image.
- This procedure is not currently needed for the truck's recorded factory camera equipment. It becomes relevant after APIM/BCM replacement, camera retrofit, a lost configuration, or relevant FORScan/FDRS programming.

## Electrical work placeholders

- Fuse and relay directory; customer access wiring and upfitter circuits; grounds; connector pinouts; trailer wiring; camera wiring; CAN network notes.
- Before programming, save full module backups and record each change, date, tool version, and recovery steps.
