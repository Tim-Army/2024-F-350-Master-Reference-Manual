How it is worked out

Bed floor = rail - bed depth. Hitched ball top = bed floor + ball height - squat. Hitched hole = hitched ball top + position offset. The trailer's nose rises (or drops) by about hitched hole - H, so hitched overhang = level overhang + (hitched hole - H). Rail clearance = hitched overhang - (rail - squat); tailgate clearance = hitched overhang - (tailgate - squat). The tilt shift is applied in full at the overhang, which sits close to the pin - a small approximation. Ratings: green meets the requirement, amber is within 1 in, red is short.

Sources: B&W RVGN31XX instructions (pn 3150-530) - hole guide and 7 in minimum; Ford fifth-wheel hitch owner's manual - 6 in minimum and the 2 in squat estimate. Measure the real trailer before relying on the result.
