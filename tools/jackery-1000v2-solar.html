On the road, the truck's 12 V outlet charges it slowly - plan on the solar or a campground outlet for a full recharge. Solar input is capped at 400 W; panels beyond that add nothing.

Estimates. Explorer 1000 v2: 1,070 Wh LiFePO4 (4,000 cycles to 70%), 1,500 W rated and 3,000 W surge AC output, 400 W max solar input, ~23.8 lb; wall charge ~1.7 hr, ~1 hr in Emergency Super Charge mode (Jackery). Usable AC energy taken as 85% of 1,070 Wh (~910 Wh) for inverter losses; solar at 80% of panel rating into the battery. Device watts are typical, not nameplate; motors and compressors start at 2-3x their running watts, and the 3,000 W surge covers short starts only. An RV roof A/C (~1,600 W running) is beyond the 1,500 W inverter.
